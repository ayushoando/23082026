# Catalog Discovery Skill

This skill allows AI agents to discover, browse, and inspect commercial office furniture manufactured by One and Only.

## Endpoints

- **Main Catalog Hub**: https://oando.co.in/products
- **Seating**: https://oando.co.in/products/seating
- **Desking & Workstations**: https://oando.co.in/products/desking
- **Collaborative Furniture**: https://oando.co.in/products/collaborative
- **Storage & Compactors**: https://oando.co.in/products/storage
- **Executive Suites**: https://oando.co.in/products/executive

## Capabilities

- Category browsing and SKU filtering
- High-resolution finishes, dimensions, and architectural specs
- Floor plan space planning BOM export

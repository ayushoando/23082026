# Product Context

## What This App Is
An office furniture catalog, configurator, and floor-planning tool for a furniture company.

## Core Features
- Furniture product catalog with categories, variants, and pricing
- Office seating and workstation configurators
- 2D floor planning and layout tool
- Client-facing product presentation
- Inventory and category management

## Business Context
- B2B commercial furniture sales (offices, corporates, tenders)
- Products include workstations, seating, storage, and modular office furniture
- Pricing is in INR; catalogs are used for client presentations and tender submissions

## Users
- Internal admin (furniture dealer team) — manages catalog, inventory, pricing
- External clients — browse catalog, configure rooms, request quotes

## Design Tone
- Professional, clean, corporate aesthetic
- Mobile-responsive but primarily desktop-used by clients
